## Verified original-source research completed

1. **Original NASA ExoMiner++ v2.4.0 inference** succeeded on **56 distinct TESS host stars** in two prior cohorts, all 56 source IDs found and corresponding original SPOC FITS processed. The original event-centered centroid companion measured **248** qualifying individual events; its initial **0.05 CCD-pixel** warning flagged zero of the 40 stars with 3+ fully observed events. [32-source run](https://github.com/DrewThomas09/ASTR_FInal_Project/actions/runs/42526063290), [24-source run](https://github.com/DrewThomas09/ASTR_FInal_Project/actions/runs/42526495887).
2. **New physical-metadata score calibration** using 57,162 original 2025 published labeled TESS TCEs and 17,206 host stars: published neural score average precision **0.9703595**; new score-only calibration **0.9694660**; new score+orbital metadata **0.9693761**. Added-physics AP difference **−0.0000899**, 350-host bootstrap 95% interval **[−0.001194,+0.000983]**. No measured score-ranking benefit. [Original-data calibrated comparison](https://github.com/DrewThomas09/ASTR_FInal_Project/actions/runs/42523223355).
3. **Development-learned centroid rule tested on new-to-student sources**: threshold **0.0016012455384686 pixels** fixed using only thirteen eligible original KP/CP stars from the prior 32-source development set, before examining the extra 24-source centroid shifts. On those 24, **five manual-review warnings**: four historically EB sources NASA had already rejected, one historically known-planet source NASA accepted at score 0.601. **No incremental accepted NASA false-positive corrections**, and potential harm from hard-vetoing a historical planet. [Source-verified 249-test comparison](https://github.com/DrewThomas09/ASTR_FInal_Project/actions/runs/42527143827).
4. **Historical source identity issue**: identical original 2025 and 2026 TCE UID strings can refer to a changed period or swapped TCE numbering. Original 2025 `tce_duration` is **DAYS** and operational NASA v2.4.0 `tce_duration` is **HOURS**. [Earlier source identity audit: 224 tests](https://github.com/DrewThomas09/ASTR_FInal_Project/actions/runs/42525161027).
5. **Additional phase- and alternative-number-aware source matching** applied to all 16 original frozen 2025 model-error stars, using official source catalogs and original 2026 released-inference outputs: **fourteen period/epoch-consistent matches**, including one assigned to a new TCE number; **one potential doubled-period alias**; **one period/epoch-incompatible fit**. A local deterministic Python script and full 16-source CSV are in the conversation final-results package. This source-identity QA is an evaluation safeguard, not a novel exoplanet detector.
6. **Engineering sensitivity check**: synthetic shifts of 0–0.05 pixels were added to already measured **individual centroid event vectors** for the seventeen previously eligible new-to-development source stars. The original 0.05-pixel threshold produced zero warnings for 0–0.01px injected shifts and twelve warnings when +0.05px was injected. This checks a separately coded event-vector warning, **NOT** injection through NASA's original raw source pixel/preprocessing/neural network pipeline. The CSV and reproducible original-code script are in the delivered package.
7. **New authentic NASA multi-model experiment completed today:** [**official single vs ten-model cross-validation ensemble run #42531853347**](https://github.com/DrewThomas09/ASTR_FInal_Project/actions/runs/42531853347), immutable official ExoMiner v2.4.0 single and `cv_ensemble` on eight frozen source TICs. Twelve NASA TCEs produced per execution; all eight prespecified matching original source UIDs and current periods/epochs comparable. **Zero of eight threshold-0.5 binary decisions changed**; maximum absolute averaged model-score change **0.4194808**. Some historical difficult cases had lower ensemble scores without crossing threshold, such as old EB TIC 165493409 (0.7465→0.5722) and old NTP TIC 354518617 (0.9433→0.5239). No per-individual-model score uncertainty is claimed without the individual member outputs. [Exact official ensemble artifact](https://github.com/DrewThomas09/ASTR_FInal_Project/actions/runs/42531853347/artifacts/13638006434).

## Intellectual conclusion

AI assisted with running, interpreting and testing sophisticated astrophysical code and with discovering data-provenance pitfalls. It did **not demonstrate a statistically supported increase in the original NASA neural model's precision, recall or exoplanet discovery rate**. The experiments do not establish training exclusion of the earlier 2025 TESS stars from NASA's 2026 model. Historical label agreements are not independent new-planet confirmations. The classroom deliverable should discuss these negative findings candidly.
